# Site-data issues found while writing the dictionary guides (2026-09-26)
Reported by the research and verification agents; NOT yet acted on unless listed under "Fixed" below. Each item names its evidence.

## Fixed (2026-09-26, local and production databases)
- **Panel dates.** `dictionaries` gained `date_approx` (shown "c. 1020") and `date_note` (replaces the plain century on the panel, the API and generator prompts). Ibn ʿAbbās: filed at c. 900, "recorded late 9th–10th c." (was 687). al-Rāghib: c. 1020, "early 11th c.; death date disputed" (was 1109). Salmoné: 1890, "published 1890" (was 1889). Mukhtār al-Ṣiḥāḥ: 1268, "d. after 1268". `sort_order` now matches for Asās 1144, Qāmūs 1415, Tāj 1791.
- **Dates inside the English.** Entry headers repeated the old stored years; brought in line: al-Rāghib 152 texts, Salmoné 13, Mukhtār 15, and the headers left behind by the earlier Asās 1143→1144 (369), Qāmūs 1414→1415 (37) and Tāj 1790→1791 (1,315) corrections. `dict_scrape.py`'s seed list now carries every corrected label and date, so a re-scrape keeps them.
- **Misfiled Ibn ʿAbbās entries moved** to the root the site's morphology gives the Qurʾanic word: 11147 xbt→xbw (خَبَتْ, Q 17:97), 9775 Ent→Enw (عَنَتِ, Q 20:111), 15503 snh→wsn (سِنَةٌ, Q 2:255). Where the source's own heading differs (ع ن ت, س ن و), the English now says so.
- **nqb (11538):** Q35:44 → Q50:36 in both English versions.
- **"Pre-Islamic" in the Ibn ʿAbbās English** removed where the poet lived into Islam or is not named: Amr, ryb (Ibn al-Zibaʿrā), zbr (Kaʿb ibn Mālik), nqE (Ḥassān, a Conquest-of-Mecca line), nHb (Labīd), ASr and Sld (Abū Ṭālib), fwm (Abū Miḥjan), xmT, rmz, Dnk, Htm, xbt. Everything below is still open unless it restates one of these.

## gharib-al-quran-fi-shir-al-arab
- Author label 'ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿAbbās' treats an attribution as authorship. The answers are attributed to Ibn ʿAbbās and the questions to Nāfiʿ ibn al-Azraq, and the collection reaches us through later chains, one of them through ʿĪsā ibn Daʾb, whom Tārīkh Baghdād (vol. 12, pp. 468, 472) reports was accused of fabrication. The label should read 'Attributed to ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿAbbās'.
- Stored date 687 is Ibn ʿAbbās's death (68 AH = 687–8 CE), not a date for the text. The recorded versions are late 9th–10th c. CE: al-Mubarrad (d. 285/898), and readings in the text's own chain dated 288/900 and 344/955 (1993 ed., pp. 25–26). Sorting the panel by 687 puts this text first, as if it were the earliest work.
- The title 'Gharīb al-Qurʾān fī Shiʿr al-ʿArab' was coined by the 1993 Beirut editors (Muḥammad ʿAbd al-Raḥīm and Aḥmad Naṣr Allāh), who say they added it to the older title (ed. p. 20). The traditional name is Masāʾil (or Suʾālāt) Nāfiʿ ibn al-Azraq.
- Misfiled entries, going by the site's own morphology table: the entry on خَبَتْ (Q 17:97) is shown on root xbt but should be on xbw. The entry on وَعَنَتِ الوجوه (Q 20:111) is shown on Ent but should be on Enw. The entry on سِنَةٌ (Q 2:255) is shown on snh but should be on wsn.
- Wrong verse number in the nqb English (both the translation and the harmonized text): 'Q35:44' should be Q 50:36. The 1993 edition, p. 183 n. 1, also gives سورة ق ٣٦. The other 48 entries' Q-references check out.
- The harmonized English for Amr and nqb calls the poetry 'pre-Islamic'. Several of the poets are mukhaḍram or Islamic-era (Labīd, Abū Miḥjan, Ḥassān, Kaʿb ibn Mālik), and 19 of the 50 displayed exchanges name no poet.
- Only 49 of the edition's 250 questions are stored. hawramani files the work by Qur'anic word, and its root pages show only some of those entries: the root pages for كثر and فلق have none, although the work has entries for الكوثر and الفلق. This limits coverage; it is not an error in the entries that are displayed.
- The stored author 'ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿAbbās' presents a disputed attribution as authorship. The work is a transmitted question-and-answer exchange: its chain runs through ʿĪsā ibn Daʾb and is dated 288 and 344 AH, and Goldziher (pp. 70–71) and Wansbrough (pp. 216–17) treat it as later. Suggest the label 'attributed to ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿAbbās'.
- The stored date 687 is Ibn ʿAbbās's death (68 AH = 687–8 CE), not the date of the text. It makes this work sort first in the panel. The earliest datable versions are late 9th–10th c. CE (al-Mubarrad d. 285/898; chain dated 288 and 344 AH). Consider showing 'attrib., d. 68/687–8'.
- Misfiled entry: snh (entry 15503, filed under سنه 'year') holds the question on سِنَةٌ 'drowsiness' (Q 2:255), whose root is و س ن; the stored heading itself reads س ن و. Likewise xbt (entry 11147) holds an entry headed خ ب و (khabat, Q 17:97). The essay already mentions the xbt case.
- The display title Gharīb al-Qurʾān fī Shiʿr al-ʿArab was added by the 1993 editors (ed1993 p. 20). The older name is Masāʾil / Suʾālāt Nāfiʿ ibn al-Azraq, which could be shown as an alternative title.
- The stored author_death_year 687 is Ibn ʿAbbās's death (68 AH = 687–8 CE), not the date of the text. The earliest datable versions are late 9th–10th c. CE (al-Mubarrad d. 285/898; chain dated 288 and 344 AH). The 687 also sorts this work first in the panel.
- Misfiled entries: snh (entry 15503) holds the question on سِنَةٌ 'drowsiness' (Q 2:255, root و س ن; the stored heading reads س ن و) on the page for سنه 'year'. xbt (entry 11147) holds the خَبَتْ question under the heading خ ب و.
- The displayed title Gharīb al-Qurʾān fī Shiʿr al-ʿArab was added by the 1993 editors (ed. p. 20). The manuscript title is Suʾālāt Nāfiʿ ibn al-Azraq, and al-Suyūṭī calls it Masāʾil Nāfiʿ ibn al-Azraq. Consider showing one of these as an alternative title.

## kitab-al-ayn
- Stored date 786 is only one of two reported death dates. al-Suyuti, al-Muzhir vol. 1 p. 66 (quoting al-Zubaydi), gives 170 AH or, in some reports, 175 AH (786-7 or 791-2 CE). The guide's period reads 'd. 170 or 175 AH / 786 or 791 CE'.
- The author label 'al-Khalil al-Farahidi' implies he wrote the work alone. The work's own introduction (Makhzumi-Samarrai ed., vol. 1 p. 48) presents it as transmitted by al-Layth ibn al-Muzaffar ('from al-Khalil, everything in this book'), and authorship has been disputed since the 3rd/9th century (al-Muzhir vol. 1 pp. 62-68). The site's English renderings routinely say 'al-Khalil notes/records' for unattributed text.
- The title romanisation 'Kitab al-ʿAin' (site) and 'Kitab al-ʿAyn' (guide and route) are the same title, كتاب العين. 'al-ʿAin' is an older romanisation, used by Haywood (1960) and hawramani. This is a variant spelling, not an error, but it is inconsistent across the site.
- The harmonized English sometimes adds interpretation that is not in the stored original. For wHy it adds a note that 'al-Khalil treats the sense of writing/inscribing as primary, then extends' it; the original only lists the senses. For Ebd it names ʿAli where the original has only 'أمير المؤمنين'.
- The stored text drops the edition's footnotes, so square-bracket additions (284 of 505 entries) and parenthesised corrections go unexplained. Some are supplied from al-Azhari's Tahdhib ('من التهذيب من أصل العين'), others 'required by context'. Chapter headings the editors supplied appear without their brackets (e.g. the كتب heading is bracketed at vol. 5 p. 341 but not in our copy). 17 entries have unbalanced brackets (e.g. حرم).
- hawramani stores each permutation chapter under the root it opens with. As a result, many Qur'anic roots treated by the work show no Kitab al-ʿAyn entry on their own root page: عمل (inside علم), رحم (inside حرم), بعد and بدع (inside عبد), بصر (صبر), بشر (شرب), جمع (عجم), حلم (حمل). A rough scan finds about 470 such embedded site roots. A cross-reference from the embedded root to its chapter would help readers.
- Source identity: hawramani names no printed edition. Collating three chapters (عبد vol. 2 pp. 48-55; كتب vol. 5 pp. 341-342; ظلم vol. 8 pp. 162-164) against the al-Makhzumi / al-Samarraʾi edition on Shamela showed they match word for word, including the editors' brackets, with footnote numbers stripped.
- The English renderings of multi-root chapters cover only the headword root. Entry 372 (Ebd): the faithful and harmonized English end at al-ʿabādīd and omit the d-ʿ-b, b-ʿ-d and b-d-ʿ sections, with no note that they are partial. Entry 1622 (Hrm): the harmonized text says it covers the ḥ-r-m section only, so raḥma and the other sections are Arabic-only. Entry 35 (Elm) does include ʿamal, so the behaviour is inconsistent; its extent has not been measured.
- The harmonized English attributes entry content directly to al-Khalīl (e.g. Ebd 'Al-Khalīl notes…', 'Al-Khalīl reads Q 43:81…'; Zlm 'al-Khalīl equates ẓulm with shirk'). Authorship and the reference of 'qāla l-Khalīl' are disputed (Muzhir vol. 1, pp. 62–67; Haywood pp. 26–27). A neutral 'the ʿAyn…' would be safer.
- The stored title spelling is 'Kitāb al-ʿAin' (following hawramani); the standard transliteration is 'al-ʿAyn', and the harmonized texts mix the two. Consider aligning.
- The stored date 786 matches al-Khalīl's death in 170 AH, one of two reported dates; 175 AH / 791 CE is also reported (Muzhir vol. 1, p. 66). It is acceptable for ordering.
- The stored text drops the editors' footnotes and some heading brackets (e.g. the ktb heading is bracketed in print at vol. 5, p. 341 but unbracketed in storage). The guide discloses this.
- English renderings of multi-root chapters cover only the headword root: Ebd (372) translation_en and harmonized_en stop at al-ʿabādīd without saying so; Hrm (1622) harmonized_en covers ḥ-r-m only; Elm (35) does cover ʿamal, so the behaviour is inconsistent.
- Harmonized English attributes the content directly to al-Khalīl (Ebd: 'Al-Khalīl notes…', 'Al-Khalīl reads Q 43:81…'; Hrm: 'Al-Khalīl reports both readings…'). Authorship and the reference of 'qāla l-Khalīl' are disputed (Muzhir vol. 1, pp. 62–67; Haywood pp. 26–27); 'the ʿAyn' would be safer.
- Stored title 'Kitāb al-ʿAin' follows hawramani; the standard transliteration is al-ʿAyn, which the guide uses. Consider aligning the site label and the harmonized headers.
- Stored author 'al-Khalīl al-Farāhīdī' does not mention al-Layth's transmission. Stored date 786 (170 AH) is one of two reported death dates; the other is 175 AH / 791 CE (Muzhir vol. 1, p. 66). Fine for ordering, but not the only date.
- Shared box wording: 'Quotations on this page come from that stored original' is not true of quotations from a work's printed introduction, which this guide uses. Consider 'Excerpts from entries come from that stored original'.
- Stored title 'Kitāb al-ʿAin' (hawramani spelling) differs from the guide's 'Kitāb al-ʿAyn'; consider aligning the site label.
- Stored author 'al-Khalīl al-Farāhīdī' omits al-Layth ibn al-Muẓaffar's transmission (ayn-intro vol. 1, p. 48) and the authorship dispute (Muzhir vol. 1, pp. 62–67).
- Stored date 786 (=170 AH) is one of two reported death dates; the other is 175 AH / 791 CE (Muzhir vol. 1, p. 66). Fine for ordering.
- Harmonized English attributes content directly to al-Khalīl ('Al-Khalīl reads Q 43:81…'), although authorship and the reference of 'qāla l-Khalīl' are disputed (Haywood p. 27; Muzhir vol. 1, p. 63); 'the ʿAyn' would be safer.
- Stored text drops the editors' footnotes, so readers cannot tell which square-bracketed words come from al-Azharī's Tahdhīb; some bracketed headings (ktb, vol. 5, p. 341) lose their brackets.

## al-muhit-fi-l-lugha
- rfq (entry_id 9061): the stored entry opens with 'رفق مُهْمَلٌ عنده. الخارزنجي: الرَّوَاذق…', but in Āl Yāsīn's printed edition (vol. 5, p. 373) this note belongs under root رذق. The harmonized English wrongly says al-Khalīl left رفق untreated. The guide's onOurSite warns readers.
- Efw (entry_id 3183): the stored text 'عفو وعفى عفى أهمله الخليل' has lost the edition's quotation marks. The print (vol. 2, p. 170) reads 'عفو عفى: «عفى» أهمله الخليل وحكاه الخارزنجي', so the note covers only the spelling عفى. Both the faithful translation and the harmonized English say al-Khalīl omitted the whole root, which is false: the Kitāb al-ʿAyn entry for ʿ-f-w is displayed on our site. The guide's onOurSite warns readers.
- kfr (entry_id 568): the harmonized English opens 'whose unifying thread is covering/concealing'. The author never says this. He ties only some senses to covering (the sun's setting-place 'because it covers it', a valley that 'covered everything along its sides', a mail-coat worn covered).
- Provenance: hawramani names no edition. Our stored text matches, almost word for word, the older al-Maktaba al-Shāmila digital text of Āl Yāsīn's edition (ʿĀlam al-Kutub, Beirut 1994, 11 vols). Checked by 40-character chunks over 15 entries: 94–100% match. It also matches the printed page where checked (vol. 10, p. 32 scan). The newer page-aligned Shamela text drops words here, e.g. 'وظلمت الشيء: نقصته، من قوله عز وجل'. Editor's footnotes and the chapter headings of the phonetic arrangement are absent from our copy.
- Stray characters and misplaced vowels in stored text, e.g. zwr 'وال! زوَرُ', sbl 'وااسابِلةُ', kfr 'ألْجَاتَ' (print: ألْجَأْتَ).
- Stored date 995 and the author label 'al-Ṣāḥib b. ʿAbbād' are correct (d. 24 Ṣafar 385 AH / 30 March 995, Rayy).
- FYI, not on our site: hawramani's page for this dictionary calls him 'a Persian Shiite scholar'. Encyclopaedia Iranica says 'Although not himself a Shiʿite'.
- Harmonized English for Muḥīṭ Zlm (entry 593) adds an interpretive heading, "'OUT OF PLACE' — CONCRETE EXTENSIONS OF ẒULM", that the original does not make. Harmonized English for Muḥīṭ kfr (entry 568) says 'whose unifying thread is covering/concealing' and glosses kaffāra as 'covers/expiates' (the original has only 'ما يكفَّر به'). Both attribute to the author a semantic unification he does not state.
- Stored Muḥīṭ rfq entry (9061) begins with 'رفق مهمل عنده. الخارزنجي: الرواذق…'. The Āl Yāsīn print (vol. 5, p. 373) places this notice under رذق, so the heading error is inherited from the older Shamela digital text.
- The stored author label (al-Ṣāḥib b. ʿAbbād) and date (995) are correct: he died on 30 March 995 at Rayy (Encyclopaedia Iranica).
- Harmonized English (default view) for Muḥīṭ Zlm (entry 593) adds the heading "'OUT OF PLACE' — CONCRETE EXTENSIONS OF ẒULM". For Muḥīṭ kfr (entry 568) it states 'whose unifying thread is covering/concealing'. The author makes neither unification. Both are still present in round 2.
- Harmonized English for Muḥīṭ grf (entry 8051) identifies 'Abū Ḥanīfa [al-Dīnawarī]'. Neither the stored original nor the Āl Yāsīn print (vol. 5, p. 67) names him.
- Stored Muḥīṭ rfq entry (9061) begins with a notice ('رفق مُهْمَلٌ عنده. الخارزنجي: الرواذق…') that the print places under the heading رذق (vol. 5, p. 373). The error is inherited from the older Shamela EPUB, which has the same misplaced heading.
- Stored label (al-Ṣāḥib b. ʿAbbād) and date (995) are correct: he died on 30 March 995 in Rayy (Iranica).

## maqayis-al-lugha
- Some roots carry a chapter that belongs to the whole letter. In several letters the source copy from hawramani (and so our stored text) runs the letter's closing chapter on words longer than three letters ('باب ما جاء من كلام العرب على أكثر من ثلاثة أحرف') into the last three-letter root printed before it. Confirmed for كفر (kfr): the chapter heading is dropped, and in Hārūn's edition the chapter starts on vol. 5 p. 193. Confirmed for جثم (jvm): the heading appears in the middle of a verse, and the text breaks off at 'ومما وضع وضعا ولم أعرف له اشتقاقا:'; in Hārūn it is vol. 1 p. 505. - Stored date 1889 is not a death year. It is the date on the preface ('21st November 1889. University College, London', vol. 1 p. x). The title page reads 'London: Trübner & Co., Ludgate Hill. 1890.' The compiler's death year is not reliably established: Wikidata gives Oct 1904 with no source, and a blog gives 1830–1895. The panel treats the stored value as a death year, so for this work it is really a publication date.
- The title label 'An Advanced Learner's Arabic-English Dictionary' is the title of a 1970s reissue (Google Books record: al-Biruni, 1976) and of the Perseus digital text. The original title is 'An Arabic-English Dictionary on a New System' (2 vols, London: Trübner, 1890). The imprint Perseus gives, which hawramani and al-nuqta inherit ('Beirut. Librairie du Liban. 1889'), pairs a Beirut publisher with the preface year and is not the imprint on the original London title page. The author label 'Habib Anthony Salmoné' agrees with the Library of Congress authority heading; the title page prints 'H. Anthony Salmoné'.
- The stored text comes from Perseus/Alpheios via hawramani. Perseus spelled out the print's pattern numbers and Roman numerals as Arabic words, and our copy then dropped those numbers and the aorist-vowel letters A/I/U while keeping cross-references to them ('see V', 'see 21 (a)'). This leaves fused references such as 'see 23tb' (print: 'see 23t.—(b)') and stray numbers such as '38'. Other leftovers, by count of displayed entries: garbled 'ڤ' forms in 197 (e.g. 'N. P. رَبڤبَ' for the print's N.P.I = مَرْبُوب), empty '[]' in 248 where the print had a pattern number, and '_ast;' in 24. Some verb forms rebuilt by machine are wrong for weak roots, e.g. صَلَّوَ for صَلَّى under Slw and زَكَّوَ for زَكَّى under zkw.
