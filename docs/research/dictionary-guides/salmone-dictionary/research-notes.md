## 4. Site data issues (for return value)
1. **Stored date 1889** is the date of the preface (21 Nov 1889), not a death year; the title-page imprint is **1890**. The compiler's death year is not reliably established (Wikidata unsourced: Oct 1904; a blog: 1895). The panel orders by stored "death year", so 1889 places Salmoné before any 1890s death date — harmless for ordering after Lane (stored 1876) but semantically a publication date.
2. **Title label** "An Advanced Learner's Arabic-English Dictionary" is the title of the 1970s reissue and of the Perseus digital text; the original 1890 title is *An Arabic-English Dictionary on a New System*. Perseus's "Beirut: Librairie du Liban, 1889" (inherited by hawramani/al-nuqta) mixes a later publisher with the preface year.
3. Author label "Habib Anthony Salmoné" is consistent with LC authority; title page prints "H. Anthony Salmoné".
4. Stored text artifacts: fused/unresolved table references ("see 23tb"), stray numbers ("38"), "ڤ" in 197 entries, empty "[]" in 248, "_ast;" in 24; mechanically generated verb forms wrong for weak roots (صَلَّوَ, زَكَّوَ); aorist-vowel letters (A/I/U) and Roman numerals dropped.
5. Some roots show only a fragment (dnw one line; nhy "see supra (c)"; brA "Created.") because the main article is under a sibling spelling (e.g. دني) not attached to the Qur'anic root.
6. Harmonized English for rbb says "English-Arabic learner's dictionary" (should be Arabic–English).
7. Guide page excerpt caption renders "From the Salmoné's dictionary entry as stored on al-nuqta" (registry label "Salmoné's dictionary" inserted after "the") — grammar glitch in shared component/registry, not in the guide file.

## 6. Open questions / evidence gaps
- No scholarly study of Salmoné's dictionary found (Haywood 1960 does not mention it; no review located in JRAS/Academy/Athenaeum searches).
