## Own computations (displayed entries, n = 1,081)

- **Match with print.** I normalized both texts to letters only (hamza forms folded) and compared every displayed entry with the whole Shamela text. All 1,081 are found, including the 14 entries with square brackets.
- **Length.** 549 entries (raw) and 555 (vowel marks stripped) are 40 characters or fewer. Median 40; mean 52.
- **Braces.** 1,069 entries have braced Qur'anic forms. By number of braced items: 1 in 702, 2 in 240, 3 in 86, 4+ in 41, none in 12.
- **One braced item, no قيل/يقال/أو.** 624 entries (58%). Of these, 561 (52%) gloss a single-word form.
- **Alternatives.** Strict (وقيل, قد قيل, قيل:, ويقال, يقال:): 110–123, depending on regex. Any قيل or يقال: 134. That is about 10–12%.
- **Named authorities** (8 entries):
  - Hyy: Sībawayh
  - sfh: Yūnus, Abū ʿUbayda, al-Farrāʾ
  - qnT: al-Farrāʾ
  - sEr: Abū ʿUbayda
  - sjl: Abū ʿUbayda, Ibn ʿAbbās
  - Ewl: al-Kisāʾī, al-Liḥyānī
  - wry: the Basrans
  - $nA: the Basrans and Kufans
- **aṣl.** 26 regex hits. About 23–24 are genuine *aṣl* notes; ASl is the root itself.
- **Glosses of two words or fewer.** The first gloss in 652/1,070 entries (61%); all braced glosses, 990/1,575 (63%).
- **Layer markers.** في التفسير appears in Ebd and Swr; في اللغة in Ebd; في الجاهلية in Hnf and mEn; في الإسلام in mEn; بلغة قريش in $rd.
- **Verse specificity of glossed forms.** I did a rough skeleton match of each single-word braced form against `verses.text_uthmani`. The skeleton is loose, so it overstates the multi-verse counts.
  - 525 forms occur in 1 verse, 319 in 2–3, 515 in 4 or more, and 40 were unmatched.
  - A further 221 braced items are multi-word phrases, which point to one verse.
  - So many glosses are verse-bound, but a large share gloss forms that recur. Some glosses are plainly general: {الكهف}: غار في الجبل; {عدن}: إقامة.
- **Printed headwords with no displayed entry.** After removing parser noise, about 50–55 remain. Examples: أيك أرم ألي أسي برزخ بكك بوس بئس تخذ ثمد حصص خاتم ذوي رجأ رفف روس روي زرب سبأ سلس سندس سرو صلل صيصي ضوز طفق طغو عتر علل عدن غري غثي قطن قيع قوو كلاء لبد لفي مطط متك مكأ مرو منو نيب نجل نمرق ندي هيهى ويل يئس.
