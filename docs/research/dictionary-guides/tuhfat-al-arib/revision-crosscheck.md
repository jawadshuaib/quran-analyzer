# tuhfat-al-arib — cross-guide consistency revision (2026-09-26)

## Changes

- [sameness] Lede, first sentence: "*Tuḥfat al-Arīb* is less a dictionary than a glossary." → "*Tuḥfat al-Arīb* is a glossary." The "less an X than a Y" opening was shared with the asas-al-balagha, lane-lexicon and lisan-al-arab ledes. The rest of the lede is unchanged.

## New claims

None. The edit only removes the contrast with "dictionary". The claim that the work is a glossary was already in the lede and is supported by the author's own description of it as a *mukhtaṣar* on *gharīb al-Qurʾān* (Tuḥfa, ed. al-Majdhūb, p. 40, cited in the body).

## Removed claims

- The implied contrast that the work is "less a dictionary" than a glossary. This was framing, not a sourced claim.
