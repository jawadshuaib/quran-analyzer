## What is new this round

I found and read a **scan of the printed Majdhūb edition** itself (archive.org item `000134_201807`, 397 pp., PDF page numbers = printed page numbers; title page: "تحفة الأريب بما في القرآن من الغريب … تحقيق سمير المجذوب … المكتب الإسلامي"; intro signed "سمير المجذوب"; imprint "الطبعة الأولى … ١٩٨٣ م"). Comparing it with the Shamela digital text shows that **the Shamela text omits the editor's footnotes and folds a few of them into square brackets of its own**. Earlier rounds assumed the square brackets were the printed editor's. They are not:

- **Print p. 272** (كون): text ends "يَنْبَاعُ مِنْ ذِفْرى غضوب حرّة (1)". No [جسرة]. Footnote (1): "وفي ظ: ينباع من ذفري والباقي ساقط وفي اللسان (بوع) قال عنترة: ينباع من ذفرى غضوب جسرة / زيافة مثل الفنيق المكدم …". The Shamela text's "[جسرة]" is therefore the digital preparers' insertion, apparently drawn from that footnote.
- **Print p. 104** (حلم): "حَلَمَ (5): . . . . . . . . . . . ." (a heading followed by a row of dots). Footnote (5): "هكذا في الأصل وظ. وردت المادة فقط وما بعدها بياض. وهي ساقطة في ب." There are **no square brackets** on the printed page; Shamela has "[حلم]".
- **Print p. 53** (أذن): "« يَأْذَنَ » (1): أعلم". Footnote (1): "في ظ: آذن". Shamela renders this as "يأذن [الصواب: آذن كما في ظ]", adding the word الصواب ("the correct form") that the editor did not write.
- **Sigla (editor's intro, "رموز التحقيق")**: "الأصل: نسخة الخزانة التيمورية – دار الكتب المصرية. ظ: نسخة المكتبة الظاهرية بدمشق. ب: نسخة المكتبة الوطنية بباريس." So ظ is confirmed as the siglum of the Ẓāhiriyya manuscript (Damascus).
- **Earlier printings (editor's intro)**: "طبع في حماه سنة ١٣٤٥ هـ / ١٩٢٦ م … الشيخ محمد سعيد بن مصطفى الوردي النعساني الحموي" and "أعيد طبع الكتاب سنة … ١٩٧٧ م. تحقيق الدكتور أحمد مطلوب والدكتورة خديجة الحديثي". Independently, archive.org `AAlexandrina-103515` (Bibliotheca Alexandrina copy) has date "1345 [1926]", publisher مطبعة الإخلاص. (Hatib p. 55 prints the Hijri year of the first printing as 1337, a slip; the guide gives only CE years, so it is unaffected.)
- **Editor's plan (intro, item 5 and 8)**: the printed footnotes give each word's sura and verse, and the editor adds "some views the author omitted … with attribution and some shawāhid". None of this is in our copy.
- **Znn footnote (print)**: the editor locates يظنون = يوقنون at Q 2:249 and gives the ẓāʾ reading of 81:24 to Ibn Kathīr, Abū ʿAmr and al-Kisāʾī. That is consistent with the guide (which says only that the gloss "fits" Q 2:46, labels the contrast as its own, and says three of the Seven read *bi-ẓanīn*).

## Flagged items and fixes

- **C36 (body, ك و ن excerpt translation).** "…an ill-tempered, noble [editor's bracket: sturdy] she-camel."
  - Evidence: print p. 272 has no bracket; the line ends "حرّة" with a footnote quoting the Lisān (ʿAntara) reading جسرة. The bracket was added in the Shamela digital text that our copy reproduces.
  - Fix: change "[editor's bracket: sturdy]" to "[added in brackets in our source text: sturdy]" (or "[a bracketed variant added in the digital text: sturdy]").
- **C67 (onOurSite).** "…is a heading with nothing under it, as in the printed text, where it stands in square brackets.[^tuhfa|p. 104]"
  - Evidence: print p. 104: "حَلَمَ: . . . . . ." and footnote (5) "هكذا في الأصل وظ. وردت المادة فقط وما بعدها بياض. وهي ساقطة في ب."
