## Changed passages

1. **Body, "Reading it for the Qur'an"**: "…from the *Kitāb al-ʿAyn*, credited to the eighth-century al-Khalīl,[^haywood|p. 20]…"
   - Source opened: Haywood, *Arabic Lexicography* (1960), full text on archive.org (Binder2_djvu.txt). The page after the "19" running head and before "21" is p. 20, the opening of Chapter Three, "Al-Khalil ibn Ahmad". It says the *Kitāb al-ʿAyn* "was ascribed to a famous scholar who had been dead for over 70 years", al-Khalīl, and dates him "100/718-19 to 170-175/786-791". p. 18 also says "towards the end of the Eighth Century".
   - "Credited to" matches Haywood's "ascribed to" and the kitab-al-ayn guide, which uses "credited to al-Khalīl" and gives his death as 170 or 175 AH / 786 or 791 CE. "Eighth-century" is correct for al-Khalīl.

   - Verdict: **SUPPORTED**.
