## Flagged items and fixes

1. **C38 (Zlm, "keeps the senses")**: NQ. Change "Ibn ʿAbbād keeps the senses and drops every poet, and the verse as well:" to "Ibn ʿAbbād keeps most of these senses but drops every poet, and that verse as well:". Entry 593 has no equivalent of the ʿAyn's ليلة ظلماء "very evil" or أظلم فلان علينا البيت.
2. **C45 ("Most entries speak impersonally")**: NQ. Change to "Many statements are impersonal, introduced by 'it is said' (*wa-qīla*) or 'they say', and his sources usually go unnamed.[^jubouri]". Jubouri says only that such phrases are frequent. In our copy وقيل occurs in 293 of 836 entries.
3. **C48 ("Such remarks are rare")**: NQ. Change to "In the entries we display such first-person remarks are rare, though a full study of the book gives a whole section to his criticism of words and of earlier lexicographers, al-Khalīl and al-Khārzanjī among them.[^jubouri]". Or at least write "rare in our copy".
4. **C52 (Lisān "with different members")**: NQ, and inaccurate as written. Replace with "The [[root:kfr|ibn-manzur-lisan-al-arab|Lisān al-ʿArab]] gives nearly the same four-part scheme, credited to 'some of the learned' (*baʿḍ ahl al-ʿilm*): denial, obstinacy and hypocrisy, with *inkār* ('rejection', defined as unbelief of heart and tongue) as the fourth." Stored Lisān kfr: "كفر إنكار … وكفر جحود، وكفر معاندة، وكفر نفاق … فأما كفر الإنكار فهو أن يكفر بقلبه ولسانه". The corrected wording shows even more clearly that this is a scholars' classification.
5. **C53 ("The rest of the entry is concrete")**: NQ. Change to "Much of the rest is concrete:". Entry 568 also has *al-kaffāra* (expiation of sin), *kāfūr* as a spring in Paradise, and *takfīr* as a *dhimmī*'s bow.

## Brief issues (not factual)

- **must-fix: the default English view contradicts the essay.** The essay says (correctly) that the Zlm entry does not use "putting a thing out of its place" to explain other senses, and that the kfr entry never says covering unites its senses. A reader who follows the link lands on the harmonized English (default view). For Zlm it has a heading "'OUT OF PLACE' — CONCRETE EXTENSIONS OF ẒULM". For kfr it opens "whose unifying thread is covering/concealing" and glosses *kaffāra* as what "covers" a fault. The essay should tell readers that these points are checked against "Original Text", not the condensed English, or the onOurSite note should warn that the English view sometimes adds groupings the author does not make. See site-data issues.
- suggestion: "Abū Ḥanīfa's original" in the grf excerpt will make most readers think of the jurist. Identify him, if verified, as the botanist Abū Ḥanīfa al-Dīnawarī (the entry is about the tanning plant *gharaf*), or add a neutral gloss such as "a copy in Abū Ḥanīfa's own hand". The guide should not assert the identification without a source.
- suggestion: in onOurSite, name the root the rfq note belongs to (ر ذ ق, *rawādhiq*) so readers are not puzzled by a cookery word under "gentleness". It is unlinked, since our copy has no entry for it.
- suggestion: at 1,097 words (lede + body) the page is at the top of the range. The biography paragraph and the kufr paragraph are dense. It is not padded, but a light trim would make it easier to read.
- suggestion: the al-yasin locator "vol. 1, pp. 15–16" for the arrangement can be tightened to p. 15.
- No generic praise, no rankings, and no forced "original root meaning" narrative (the essay explicitly declines to read *aṣl* as a unifying theory). Every root mention is linked, translations are labelled, and the interpretation is flagged ("In our reading", "By our rough count").

## Site-data issues

1. Harmonized English for Muḥīṭ Zlm (entry 593) adds an interpretive grouping, "'OUT OF PLACE' — CONCRETE EXTENSIONS OF ẒULM", that the original does not make. Harmonized English for Muḥīṭ kfr (entry 568) states "whose unifying thread is covering/concealing" and glosses *kaffāra* as "covers/expiates", where the original has only "ما يكفَّر به". Both attribute to the author a semantic unification he does not state.
2. The stored rfq entry (9061) begins with a notice, "رفق مهمل عنده. الخارزنجي: الرواذق…", that the Āl Yāsīn print places under رذق (5:373). The stored text inherits a heading error from the older Shamela digital text.
3. The stored label (al-Ṣāḥib b. ʿAbbād) and date (995) are correct: he died 30 March 995 (Iranica).
