# Studio Rubric — self-rating video candidates

Every mined candidate is scored BEFORE any drafting happens. Drafting is
expensive and the review queue is precious; the rubric's job is to make
sure only ideas worth the operator's attention become scripts.

## Procedure (non-negotiable order)

1. Write the **rationale first** — 2–3 sentences arguing the score against
   the criteria below. Scoring before writing the rationale produces
   grade inflation; the argument must come first.
2. Then assign 0–2 per criterion, sum to 0–10.
3. **Kill below 8.** Record the kill with its rationale (`rejected_score`)
   so the idea is never proposed again.

## The five criteria (0–2 each)

1. **Surprise** — would someone who has read the Qur'an their whole life
   learn something? 2 = genuinely new lens; 1 = fresh framing of a known
   point; 0 = anyone could have said it.
2. **One-glance concreteness** — is the payoff a SPECIFIC word or verse
   that can be highlighted on screen? 2 = one word carries the whole
   insight; 1 = a verse carries it; 0 = the insight is abstract
   commentary (these die at the match gate anyway — kill them here).
3. **Cold hook** — can a stranger with zero context care within 5
   seconds, in plain English, no Arabic, no jargon? Write the candidate
   hook sketch and judge THAT, not the topic's potential.
4. **Payoff density** — does the insight fill 45–75s without padding, or
   earn ~2 min with a genuine second act? 0 if it's a one-liner stretched
   or a lecture compressed.
5. **Integrity** — is every load-bearing claim already IN the corpus
   (exegesis note, lexicon, comparison, judged meaning) verbatim — no
   synthesis stretch, no "probably", no imported tradition? 0 if the
   drafting step would need to invent anything.

## Series-specific sharpeners

- **qa**: the question itself must pass criterion 3 — if the question
  needs rephrasing to hook, rate the REPHRASED hook.
- **exegesis**: the note must anchor to a visible word/structure
  (criterion 2). Notes that are pure thematic commentary: kill.
- **root**: there must be a verse where the divergence BITES — where the
  root-based meaning changes what the verse says to the viewer. The
  lexicon story alone is not a video.
- **poetry**: the bayt must be quotable (translated, self-contained) and
  the shift must be nameable in one sentence ("poets used it for X; the
  Qur'an aimed it at Y").

## Anti-patterns (automatic 0 on the relevant criterion)

- Hooks that ask "did you know…?" or promise "the amazing meaning of…"
- Insights whose payoff is a generality ("God is merciful")
- Any candidate needing post-Quranic framing to land
- A second verse that repeats rather than advances (fails density)
- Roots where the "divergence" is a synonym (connection/bond/tie — fine
  shades don't stop a scroll)
